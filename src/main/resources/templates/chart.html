How it works

Built with Weka and ta4j on Binance OHLC candles.

The models

Linear regression and Random Forest models read 20 indicators on each of the 1D, 1W and 1M timeframes.

More
  • Training samples: how many historical candles the model learned from.
  • The track record only counts forecasts made by a model trained on data from before that forecast.

Wyckoff phases

Accumulation and markup lean bullish; distribution and markdown lean bearish.

More
  • Detected with the Law of Effort vs Result (volume vs price spread) and VWAP, to spot institutional buying or selling.

Signals

Each signal bar points right when it favours a rise and left when it favours a fall.

More
  • Trend: 5-period vs 20-period average. Momentum: price change over the last 10 (daily) or 5 candles.
  • RSI: below 50 counts as room to rise, above 50 as stretched.
  • Fear & Greed and funding are read contrarian: extreme greed or crowded longs count against a rise.
  • Mean reversion combines those two, from −1 to +1.
  • MACD, Bollinger Bands, SMAs and volume profile feed the models too.

Volatility & money flow

Volatility shows how wild the market is; money flow whether volume is buying or selling.

More
  • Volatility: 20-period price variance. Low suggests coiling; high suggests sharp moves and shakeouts. The threshold depends on the timeframe.
  • Money flow: Chaikin Money Flow. Above 0 is aggressive buying, below 0 aggressive selling.

This is an experiment, not financial advice. The models are probabilistic and past accuracy doesn't guarantee future results.
